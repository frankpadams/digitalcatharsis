# Digital Catharsis

An early, browse-first prototype for mental health resources serving Deaf, DeafBlind, and hard of hearing communities.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser; no build process needed.

## Current state

- Responsive, accessible topic browsing and location browsing.
- **Starter outbound resource links**, not a vetted video catalog or completed provider directory. Video source cards deliberately do not claim to be individual verified ASL videos.
- No live deployment or custom domain is configured.

## Next milestones

1. Research and manually verify individual ASL video URLs, creators, captions, topic coverage and embedding permissions; replace source cards with real video previews.
2. Expand and verify organization/provider records, especially communication accessibility, credentials, eligibility and state-specific coverage.
3. Support all states, map-based or simple state browsing, and an editorial review process.
4. Test with Deaf ASL users and DeafBlind users before launch.
5. Connect to hosting without changing the existing DigitalCatharsis.net site until approved.

This prototype does not provide clinical advice, clinical referrals or emergency assistance.
